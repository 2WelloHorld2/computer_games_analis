# 🎮 Video Game Sales Analysis

**Exploratory data analysis · Market trends · Statistical testing**

## Overview
Analysis of historical video game sales for a fictional online retailer. The project explores how sales vary across platforms, genres, and markets to inform product assortment and marketing choices.

**[View the Jupyter notebook](project/main.ipynb)**

## Business questions
- Which gaming platforms were most relevant in different periods?
- How do sales distributions differ across genres and regions?
- Are critic or user ratings associated with commercial performance?
- Which trends may be useful for planning future marketing efforts?

## Workflow
1. Inspect and clean historical game data.
2. Explore sales trends by year, platform, and genre.
3. Visualize distributions and relationships between variables.
4. Compare markets and run statistical hypothesis tests.
5. Summarize patterns and limitations for a business audience.

## Stack
`Python` · `pandas` · `NumPy` · `Matplotlib` · `Seaborn` · `SciPy`

## Deliverable
A reproducible notebook with exploratory charts, statistical comparisons, and discussion of the trends observed in the historical dataset. These associations are descriptive and do not guarantee future sales.
